## Transit Atlas

**Discover more. Travel with confidence.**

Transit Atlas is an independent public transportation explorer designed for Southern New England.

Whether you're planning a familiar commute or discovering somewhere new, Transit Atlas is being built around one simple idea: essential travel information should remain useful even when your internet connection disappears.

**Explore the network**
- Browse public transportation routes and stops.
- Discover connections between communities.
- Explore regional bus and passenger rail networks.
- Find new places to visit using public transit.

**Designed for offline travel**
- Store supported transit data directly on your device.
- Browse downloaded routes without an internet connection.
- See where schedule coverage is available or incomplete.
- Keep essential information accessible while traveling.

**Built for real journeys**

Transit Atlas is being developed to support connecting journeys, return-trip planning, walking and scooter connections, and offline maps.

**Initial regional focus**

Connecticut and the Pioneer Valley Transit Authority (PVTA) network in western Massachusetts, with broader Southern New England coverage planned.

**Independent and transparent**

Transit Atlas is not affiliated with CTtransit, PVTA, Amtrak, or any other transportation agency. Published schedules may change, and not all services are available in the application.

Transit Atlas is an evolving project. Check official operators for current service information, disruptions, and important connections.

It does not yet provide full offline routing, offline maps, native GPS, complete CT/PVTA coverage, or production data-pack installation.
