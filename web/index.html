TRUST THE SOURCE, KNOW THE LIMITS

What’s behind the map.

Official published schedules are distinct from live operation. Source dates and validation issues stay visible.

Coverage is incomplete. PVTA is loaded; Holyoke, Chicopee and Westfield bus journeys can be assessed within its published service period. Other unlisted agencies are also missing. MBTA coverage is commuter rail only.

Source hierarchy

Official current GTFS → official structured schedules → reviewed official PDFs → official HTML → historical archives (reference only).

Unreviewed PDF extraction never enters the planner. Geographic transfers are estimates; no street-level route is inferred from a PDF timetable. There are no scheduled refresh jobs.
